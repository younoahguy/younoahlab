Why does ice float?

Ice is lighter than the same amount of liquid water. Water is one of the very few things that gets bigger when it freezes.

How it works

  1. When water freezes, its molecules lock into a pattern of open rings.
  2. The rings leave more empty space than in liquid water, so ice takes up about 9% more room.
  3. More room for the same weight means ice is less dense, and less dense things float.
  4. In fresh water, about 92% of a floating piece of ice sits below the surface.

The numbers

  • Ice0.917 grams per cubic centimeter
  • Liquid water1.000 grams per cubic centimeter
  • Water is heaviest at4 °C

How do we know?

You can test it at home: weigh a block of ice, then measure how much water it pushes aside. This rule was worked out by Archimedes more than 2,200 years ago.
