How does rain form?

The Sun lifts water into the air as a gas. High up it cools back into tiny drops, and when the drops get heavy enough, they fall.

How it works

  1. Heat from the Sun turns water from seas, lakes and plants into water vapor.
  2. The vapor rises, cools and turns into tiny droplets around specks of dust. That is a cloud.
  3. Droplets bump together and grow.
  4. When they are too heavy for the air to hold up, they fall as rain, and the cycle starts again.

The numbers

  • Cloud dropletabout 0.02 mm wide
  • Raindropabout 2 mm wide

How do we know?

A raindrop holds about as much water as a million cloud droplets. Weather scientists track clouds and rain with radar and satellites.
