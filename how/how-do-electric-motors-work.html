How do electric motors work?

A motor uses electricity to make a magnet, and that magnet is pushed and pulled so it spins.

How it works

  1. Electricity flowing through a coil of wire turns the coil into a magnet.
  2. Fixed magnets around the coil push and pull on it, so it turns.
  3. Just as it lines up, the motor flips the current so the coil keeps getting pushed around.
  4. Run a motor backward, by spinning it, and it makes electricity. That is a generator.

How do we know?

Michael Faraday showed in 1821 that electricity and magnets can make things move, and in 1831 that a moving magnet can make electricity. Fans, electric cars and washing machines all use his discovery.
