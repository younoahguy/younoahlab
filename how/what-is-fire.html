How It Works · Matter

What is fire?

Fire is a fast chemical reaction called burning. Fuel joins with oxygen from the air and gives off heat and light.

How it works

  1. Fire needs three things: fuel, oxygen and heat. Take one away and it goes out.
  2. Heat turns part of the fuel into gas, and that gas reacts with oxygen.
  3. The reaction makes carbon dioxide, water vapor and a lot of energy.
  4. A flame is hot, glowing gas. The yellow color comes from tiny glowing bits of soot.

The numbers

  • Hottest part of a candle flameabout 1,400 °C
  • Oxygen in airabout 21%

How do we know?

In the 1770s Antoine Lavoisier weighed things very carefully before and after burning. He showed that burning uses up a gas from the air, and he named that gas oxygen.
