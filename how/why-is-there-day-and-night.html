Why do we have day and night?

Earth spins like a top. The side facing the Sun has day, and the side facing away has night.

How it works

  1. Earth turns around once about every 24 hours.
  2. As your spot turns toward the Sun, you see sunrise.
  3. As it turns away, you see sunset.
  4. The Sun is not really moving across the sky. You are the one moving.

The numbers

  • One spinabout 24 hours
  • Speed at the equatorabout 1,670 km/h

How do we know?

In 1851 Léon Foucault hung a heavy pendulum from a tall ceiling. Its swing slowly turned during the day, showing that the Earth was rotating underneath it.
