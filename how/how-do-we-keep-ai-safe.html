How do people keep AI safe?

People set rules for what an AI may do, test it carefully, and keep a human in charge of big decisions.

How it works

  1. Rules called guardrails limit what the AI is allowed to do and which tools it can use.
  2. For anything that costs money or cannot be undone, the AI asks a person first. This is called keeping a human in the loop.
  3. Teams test the AI on purpose with tricky cases before other people use it.
  4. A trustworthy AI is honest: it says it is an AI, and it says when it is not sure.

How do we know?

Run the pizza mission in the AI & Agents lab with and without the ask-a-human rule and see the difference. For you, the safe habits are simple: never share personal details with an AI, and ask a grown-up when something feels wrong.
