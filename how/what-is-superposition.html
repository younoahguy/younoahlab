What is superposition?

Superposition means a quantum thing can be in a mix of possibilities at once, and only gives one definite answer when it is measured.

How it works

  1. A normal coin lying on a table is heads or tails.
  2. A quantum bit, or qubit, can be in a blend of 0 and 1, with a certain chance of each.
  3. When you measure it, you get just one answer, 0 or 1.
  4. After measuring, the blend is gone and the qubit stays at the answer you got.

How do we know?

Measure one qubit and the result looks random. Measure thousands prepared the same way and the split matches the predicted chances every time. Try it in the Quantum lab with the Measure 100 button. Schrödinger's cat is a famous thought experiment about this idea, not a real cat.
