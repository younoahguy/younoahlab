What causes earthquakes?

Earth's outer shell is broken into giant plates that slowly move. When two plates get stuck and then suddenly slip, the ground shakes.

How it works

  1. The plates move a few centimeters each year, about as fast as your fingernails grow.
  2. Where plates meet, their rough edges catch and get stuck.
  3. Stress builds up for years, like bending a stick.
  4. When the rock finally snaps and slips, waves of energy race out through the ground.

The numbers

  • Plate speeda few centimeters per year
  • One step up in magnitudeabout 32 times more energy

How do we know?

Instruments called seismometers record the shaking all over the world. By comparing the arrival times at different stations, scientists can pinpoint where an earthquake started.
