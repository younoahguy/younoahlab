How does gravity work?

Gravity is a pull between anything that has mass. The more mass something has, and the closer you are to it, the stronger the pull.

How it works

  1. Earth has a huge mass, so it pulls everything toward its center. That is why things fall down.
  2. Falling things speed up by 9.8 meters per second every second.
  3. Without air in the way, a hammer and a feather fall at exactly the same rate.
  4. Albert Einstein showed that mass bends space and time, and things follow those bends.

The numbers

  • Gravity on Earth9.8 m/s²
  • Gravity on the Moon1.62 m/s²

How do we know?

In 1971 astronaut David Scott dropped a hammer and a feather on the Moon, where there is no air. They landed at the same moment, just as Galileo had predicted about 400 years earlier.
