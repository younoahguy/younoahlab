Why does the Moon change shape?

The Moon does not really change shape. Half of it is always lit by the Sun, and we see different amounts of that lit half as it goes around Earth.

How it works

  1. The Moon gives off no light of its own. It shines because sunlight bounces off it.
  2. The Moon travels around Earth about once a month.
  3. When it is on the far side of Earth from the Sun, we see its whole lit face: a full moon.
  4. When it is between us and the Sun, the lit side faces away and we see almost nothing: a new moon.

The numbers

  • Full moon to full moonabout 29.5 days
  • Distance to the Moonabout 384,400 km

How do we know?

Try it with a lamp and a ball in a dark room. Hold the ball at arm's length and turn in a circle, and you will see every phase.
