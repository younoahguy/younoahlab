How It Works · Electricity

How do light bulbs work?

Old bulbs heat a thin wire until it glows. LED bulbs make light directly when electricity passes through a special material.

How it works

  1. In an old-style bulb, electricity is forced through a very thin tungsten wire.
  2. The wire gets so hot, about 2,500 °C, that it glows white.
  3. Most of that energy turns into heat, not light.
  4. In an LED, electrons drop to a lower energy level inside a tiny chip and give off light, with far less heat wasted.

The numbers

  • LEDs useat least 75% less energy
  • Old bulbs waste as heatabout 90%

How do we know?

You can feel the difference: an old-style bulb gets too hot to touch, while an LED bulb giving the same light stays much cooler. Electricity meters show the saving.
