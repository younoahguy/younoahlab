How does the Sun shine?

The Sun squeezes hydrogen atoms together so hard that they join into helium. That gives off a huge amount of energy as light and heat.

How it works

  1. The Sun's own gravity squashes its center to about 15 million °C.
  2. At that heat and pressure, hydrogen nuclei smash together and fuse.
  3. Each fusion turns a tiny bit of mass into energy.
  4. The energy slowly works its way to the surface and shines out into space.

The numbers

  • Sunlight reaches Earth inabout 8 minutes 20 seconds
  • Age of the Sunabout 4.6 billion years

How do we know?

Scientists catch ghostly particles called neutrinos that are made by fusion in the Sun's core. The number they catch matches what fusion predicts.
