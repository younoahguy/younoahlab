Why is the sky blue?

Sunlight is all the colors mixed together. Air scatters blue light much more than red light, so blue reaches your eyes from every part of the sky.

How it works

  1. Light travels as waves, and each color has a different wavelength.
  2. Air molecules are far smaller than light waves, and they bounce short blue waves around the most.
  3. That scattered blue light comes at you from all directions.
  4. At sunset the light crosses much more air, the blue is scattered away, and red and orange are left.

The numbers

  • Blue light is scatteredseveral times more than red

How do we know?

John Tyndall and Lord Rayleigh measured this in the 1800s. You can copy it by shining a flashlight through water with a few drops of milk in it.
