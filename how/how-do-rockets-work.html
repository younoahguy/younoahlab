How do rockets work?

A rocket throws hot gas out of its back end very fast. Pushing the gas backward pushes the rocket forward.

How it works

  1. Fuel burns inside the engine and makes very hot gas.
  2. The gas rushes out of the nozzle, and the rocket is pushed the other way.
  3. Rockets do not push against the ground or the air, so they work in empty space.
  4. Space has no air to burn fuel with, so rockets carry their own oxygen.

The numbers

  • Speed to stay in low orbitabout 28,000 km/h
  • Speed to leave Earth for goodabout 40,000 km/h

How do we know?

You can feel the same push by letting go of a blown-up balloon. Engineers measure a rocket engine's push on a test stand before it ever flies.
