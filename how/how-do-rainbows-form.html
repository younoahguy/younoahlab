How do rainbows form?

Raindrops bend sunlight and split it into its colors. Millions of drops together paint an arc in the sky.

How it works

  1. Sunlight goes into a raindrop and bends.
  2. It bounces off the back of the drop and bends again on the way out.
  3. Each color bends by a slightly different amount, so the colors spread apart.
  4. You only see a rainbow with the Sun behind you and rain in front of you.

The numbers

  • Angle of a rainbowabout 42°
  • Order of colorsred on the outside, violet inside

How do we know?

Isaac Newton split sunlight with a glass prism in the 1660s and then put the colors back together into white light. You can do the same in the Light lab.
