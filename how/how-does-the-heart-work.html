How does the heart work?

Your heart is a muscle that pumps blood around your body. The blood carries oxygen and food to every cell.

How it works

  1. The heart has four rooms called chambers.
  2. The right side pumps blood to your lungs to pick up oxygen.
  3. The left side pumps that fresh blood out to the rest of your body.
  4. Little flaps called valves keep the blood moving one way. Their closing makes the lub-dub sound.

The numbers

  • Beats in a dayabout 100,000
  • Resting heart rate for school-age kidsabout 70 to 100 beats a minute

How do we know?

William Harvey showed in 1628 that blood travels in a loop around the body. You can feel your own pulse at your wrist and count the beats.
