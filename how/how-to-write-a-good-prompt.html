How do you write a good prompt?

A prompt is the instruction you give an AI. The clearer you are about what you want, the more useful the answer will be.

How it works

  1. Say the task clearly: what do you want it to do?
  2. Say who it is for, such as a 9-year-old or a science fair judge.
  3. Say the shape you want: a list, three steps, 100 words.
  4. Add an example if you can, ask it to say when it is not sure, and try again with changes if the first answer is not right.

The numbers

  • Useful ingredientsrole, task, audience, format, example, check

How do we know?

Try the same question twice in the Prompt Workshop, once vague and once with every ingredient, and compare. Never put your name, address, school or passwords in a prompt.
