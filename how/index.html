How It Works

64 big questions, answered step by step: what happens, the real numbers, and how scientists know.

Matter

  • What is an atom?An atom is the smallest piece of an element that still acts like that element. Everything you can touch is made of atoms.
  • Why are some things solid, liquid or gas?It depends on how fast the tiny particles inside are moving. Heat makes them move faster, and that changes how they hold together.
  • Why does ice float?Ice is lighter than the same amount of liquid water. Water is one of the very few things that gets bigger when it freezes.
  • What is fire?Fire is a fast chemical reaction called burning. Fuel joins with oxygen from the air and gives off heat and light.
  • Why does metal rust?Rust forms when iron reacts with oxygen and water. The iron slowly turns into a crumbly red-brown material called iron oxide.
  • How does soap clean?A soap molecule has one end that loves water and one end that loves oil. It grabs greasy dirt and lets water carry it away.

Forces

  • How does gravity work?Gravity is a pull between anything that has mass. The more mass something has, and the closer you are to it, the stronger the pull.
  • Why do some things float and others sink?Something floats if it is less dense than the liquid it sits in. Dense means how much stuff is packed into a certain space.
  • How do airplanes fly?A wing is shaped and tilted so it pushes air downward. The air pushes the wing back up, and that push is called lift.
  • How do rockets work?A rocket throws hot gas out of its back end very fast. Pushing the gas backward pushes the rocket forward.
  • What is friction?Friction is a force that slows things down when they rub against each other.
  • Why does a thrown ball curve back down?Gravity only pulls down. A thrown ball keeps moving sideways while it also falls, and the two motions together make a curve.

Electricity

  • How does electricity work?Electricity is tiny particles called electrons flowing through a material, usually a metal wire.
  • How do batteries work?A battery uses a chemical reaction to push electrons out of one end and pull them back in at the other.
  • How do magnets work?Inside a magnet, billions of atoms act like tiny magnets that all point the same way, so their pulls add up.
  • How do electric motors work?A motor uses electricity to make a magnet, and that magnet is pushed and pulled so it spins.
  • What is lightning?Lightning is a giant spark of electricity that jumps between a storm cloud and the ground, or between clouds.
  • How do light bulbs work?Old bulbs heat a thin wire until it glows. LED bulbs make light directly when electricity passes through a special material.

Light & Sound

  • Why is the sky blue?Sunlight is all the colors mixed together. Air scatters blue light much more than red light, so blue reaches your eyes from every part of the sky.
  • How do rainbows form?Raindrops bend sunlight and split it into its colors. Millions of drops together paint an arc in the sky.
  • How do we see color?Things soak up some colors of light and bounce others back. Your eyes catch the bounced light and your brain names the color.
  • How does sound travel?Sound is a vibration. Something shakes, it pushes the air next to it, and that push travels outward as a wave.
  • How do glasses and lenses work?A lens is a curved piece of glass or plastic that bends light. Bending it the right way makes a blurry picture sharp.
  • What is an echo?An echo is sound that bounces off something and comes back to you a moment later.

Earth

  • Why do we have seasons?Earth is tilted. As it goes around the Sun, first one half and then the other leans toward the Sun.
  • How does rain form?The Sun lifts water into the air as a gas. High up it cools back into tiny drops, and when the drops get heavy enough, they fall.
  • How do volcanoes erupt?Melted rock called magma is lighter than the solid rock around it, so it rises. Gas trapped inside it pushes it out.
  • What causes earthquakes?Earth's outer shell is broken into giant plates that slowly move. When two plates get stuck and then suddenly slip, the ground shakes.
  • Why is the ocean salty?Rain slowly dissolves tiny amounts of salt out of rocks, and rivers carry it to the sea. The water leaves again, but the salt stays.
  • What causes tides?The Moon's gravity pulls on Earth's oceans and makes them bulge. As Earth spins, the bulges pass by and the sea rises and falls.

Space

  • Why does the Moon change shape?The Moon does not really change shape. Half of it is always lit by the Sun, and we see different amounts of that lit half as it goes around Earth.
  • How does the Sun shine?The Sun squeezes hydrogen atoms together so hard that they join into helium. That gives off a huge amount of energy as light and heat.
  • What is a black hole?A black hole is a place where gravity is so strong that nothing can escape, not even light.
  • Why do planets go around the Sun?A planet is always falling toward the Sun, but it is also moving sideways so fast that it keeps missing. That endless falling-around is an orbit.
  • What is a star?A star is a giant ball of very hot, glowing gas that makes its own light. Our Sun is a star.
  • Why do we have day and night?Earth spins like a top. The side facing the Sun has day, and the side facing away has night.

Life

  • What is DNA?DNA is the instruction book inside almost every living cell. It tells the cell how to build and run a living thing.
  • What is a cell?A cell is the smallest living building block. Every plant and animal, including you, is made of cells.
  • How do plants make their own food?Plants use sunlight to turn water and carbon dioxide into sugar. This is called photosynthesis.
  • How does the heart work?Your heart is a muscle that pumps blood around your body. The blood carries oxygen and food to every cell.
  • How do we breathe?A muscle under your lungs pulls down and air rushes in. Your lungs pass oxygen from the air into your blood.
  • How does your body fight germs?Your body has a defense team called the immune system. It spots germs, attacks them and remembers them for next time.

Technology

  • How do computers work?A computer follows lists of instructions very, very fast. Inside, everything is stored as just two values: on and off.
  • How does the internet work?The internet is millions of computers joined together so they can pass messages to each other.
  • How does GPS know where you are?GPS satellites keep sending out the exact time. Your phone measures how long each signal took to arrive and works out where it must be.
  • How does AI work, and what is NOAH?AI is a computer program that learns patterns from huge numbers of examples. It is very good at spotting and copying patterns, but it is not alive.
  • How do touchscreens work?A touchscreen has an invisible grid that holds a tiny electric charge. Your finger changes the charge where it touches, and the phone works out the spot.
  • How do solar panels work?Solar panels turn sunlight straight into electricity. Light knocks electrons loose inside the panel, and they flow out as a current.

AI & Robots

  • How do robots work?A robot is a machine that senses the world, decides what to do, and then moves. It repeats those three steps over and over.
  • What is an algorithm?An algorithm is a list of clear steps for getting a job done, like a recipe. Computers and robots follow algorithms exactly.
  • What is machine learning?Machine learning is when a computer works out a pattern from lots of examples, instead of being told every rule by a person.
  • What is an AI agent?An AI agent is an AI that is given a goal and some tools, and then takes steps on its own to reach the goal.
  • How do AI agents use tools and work in teams?A tool is something an agent can use to find out or do something, like a search, a calculator or a calendar. Without the right tool, an agent can only guess.
  • How do you write a good prompt?A prompt is the instruction you give an AI. The clearer you are about what you want, the more useful the answer will be.
  • Why does AI sometimes get things wrong?A language AI builds its answer by predicting words that are likely to fit. It does not look facts up unless it has a tool for that, so it can sound sure and still be wrong.
  • How do people keep AI safe?People set rules for what an AI may do, test it carefully, and keep a human in charge of big decisions.
  • What is superintelligence?Superintelligence is an idea: an AI that would be far better than people at almost every kind of thinking. It does not exist today.
  • How do self-driving cars see the road?They use cameras, radar and laser scanners to measure what is around them, and a computer that turns those measurements into a map many times a second.

Quantum

  • What is quantum physics?Quantum physics is the set of rules for the very smallest things, like atoms, electrons and particles of light. Those rules are different from the ones we see every day.
  • What is the double-slit experiment?Fire tiny particles one at a time at a wall with two slits and they build up a pattern of stripes, as if each one went through both slits like a wave.
  • What is superposition?Superposition means a quantum thing can be in a mix of possibilities at once, and only gives one definite answer when it is measured.
  • What is quantum entanglement?Entanglement links two particles so that their measurement results are connected, even when they are far apart.
  • How do quantum computers work?A quantum computer uses qubits, which can be in a mix of 0 and 1 and can be entangled with each other. That lets it solve certain special problems in far fewer steps.
  • Is light a wave or a particle?Light is both. It spreads out and overlaps like a wave, but it always arrives in tiny packets called photons.
