How do plants make their own food?

Plants use sunlight to turn water and carbon dioxide into sugar. This is called photosynthesis.

How it works

  1. Roots take up water, and leaves take in carbon dioxide from the air.
  2. A green chemical in leaves called chlorophyll catches energy from sunlight.
  3. The plant uses that energy to build sugar, which is its food.
  4. Oxygen is left over, and the plant lets it out into the air for us to breathe.

The numbers

  • The recipecarbon dioxide + water + light → sugar + oxygen

How do we know?

In the 1770s Joseph Priestley found that a mouse in a sealed jar lived much longer when a mint plant was in there too. The plant was making oxygen.
