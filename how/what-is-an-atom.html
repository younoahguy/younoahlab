What is an atom?

An atom is the smallest piece of an element that still acts like that element. Everything you can touch is made of atoms.

How it works

  1. In the middle is a tiny nucleus made of protons and neutrons.
  2. Electrons zip around the nucleus in a cloud.
  3. The number of protons decides which element it is: 1 is hydrogen, 6 is carbon, 79 is gold.
  4. An atom is almost all empty space. If the nucleus were a marble in the middle of a stadium, the electrons would be up in the far seats.

The numbers

  • How wide an atom isabout 0.1 to 0.5 nanometers

How do we know?

Nobody can see an atom with their eyes. Around 1910, scientists working with Ernest Rutherford fired tiny particles at gold foil and most went straight through, which showed atoms are mostly empty space. Today special microscopes can feel single atoms and draw pictures of them.
