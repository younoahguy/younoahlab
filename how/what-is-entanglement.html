What is quantum entanglement?

Entanglement links two particles so that their measurement results are connected, even when they are far apart.

How it works

  1. Two particles are prepared together in a special way.
  2. Each one alone gives a random result, like a coin flip.
  3. Compare the two and the results always match up in a fixed way.
  4. It cannot be used to send messages faster than light, because each side only sees random results until they compare notes.

How do we know?

Albert Einstein called it spooky action at a distance and doubted it. Experiments beginning in the 1970s and 1980s showed that it is real, and the 2022 Nobel Prize in Physics was awarded for that work.
