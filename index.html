What is You Noah Lab?

You Noah Lab is an interactive science lab for kids that runs in any web browser. A friendly AI guide named NOAH lives inside it. Kids run real experiments in chemistry, physics, biology and more, and every result is calculated live from real equations and published reference data.

13 hands-on science labs

  • Element Explorer: the full periodic table of all 118 elements, with real data on every one.
  • Sound Studio: make real sound waves, see them on an oscilloscope and play a keyboard.
  • Circuits: build electric circuits with batteries, bulbs and resistors using Ohm's law.
  • Chemistry Bench: mix kitchen-safe reactions, test pH and try flame tests.
  • Light & Optics: split white light with a prism and trace rays through a lens.
  • Matter & Plasma: heat a substance from solid to liquid to gas to plasma.
  • Magnet Lab: explore magnetic fields, a compass and electromagnets.
  • Crystals & Metals: grow crystal lattices and compare real metals.
  • DNA & Genetics: build DNA, translate it into a protein and use Punnett squares.
  • Human Body: measure your own reaction time and explore heart rate.
  • Nature: food webs, photosynthesis, the water cycle and growing a plant.
  • Mad Ideas Lab: make a bold guess, then test it against real physics.
  • Lab Journal: record findings and print a lab report and certificate.

Questions grown-ups ask

Who is NOAH?

NOAH is the lab's AI guide: a computer program, not a person. He answers from science data that has been checked, opens labs on request, and says so plainly when he does not know something.

What ages is it for?

It is built for curious kids of about 7 to 13. Big Kid Mode shows the real formulas for older students.

Is the science real?

Yes. Results come from standard equations and reference values such as IUPAC atomic weights. Where a simulation is simplified, the lab says so.

What information does it collect?

None. There are no ads and no trackers, and you do not need an account to use the labs. A journal is saved only as a file on your own device. See the privacy page.

What devices does it work on?

Phones, tablets, Chromebooks and computers with a modern web browser. Nothing needs to be installed.

💧 The Water Cycle

Water never runs out — it just keeps traveling this loop forever: 🌊 Evaporation (sun lifts water as vapor) → ☁️ Condensation (vapor cools into clouds) → 🌧️ Precipitation (rain & snow fall) → 🏞️ Collection (back to rivers & seas). The water you drank today is billions of years old!
