Privacy at You Noah Lab

Last updated November 18, 2026. Draft for legal review.

The short version

You Noah Lab does not collect, store or sell personal information. There are no ads, no trackers and no cookies, and no account is needed to use the labs.

What stays on your device

Experiments, badges, the Lab Journal and anything typed to NOAH stay in the browser tab. Closing the tab clears them unless you choose to download your journal as a file to your own device.

NOAH's answers

NOAH's built-in answers are calculated on your device from data that ships with the page. By default, questions are not sent anywhere.

Live answers (optional, off by default)

A grown-up can turn on live answers for one visit. While it is on, a question NOAH cannot answer from built-in data is sent through our server (hosted by Supabase) to an AI service (Anthropic) to write a short reply. Only the typed question is sent, with no name, account or cookie, and we do not store or log questions. Like any internet request, it comes from your device's internet address; we do not use that to identify or track anyone. AI answers can be wrong and are labelled. Live answers switch off again when the page is closed. Please do not let children type personal details.

NOAH's voice

When sound is on, NOAH's own spoken lines (for example a science fact or a hello) are sent to a voice service so they can be turned into speech. The service is run for us by Supabase (hosting) and OpenAI (speech). What you type or say is never sent to it, and math answers are spoken by your device instead. We do not send names, accounts or cookies. Like any internet request, it reaches those services from your device's internet address; we do not use that to identify or track anyone. If the voice service is unavailable, NOAH uses your device's built-in voice. Turn sound off and nothing is sent at all.

Voice and microphone

Talking to NOAH is optional and off until a grown-up agrees. Speech is turned into text by your web browser. Some browsers send audio to the browser maker's servers to do this. You Noah Lab itself never records, stores or sends audio. Spoken replies use your device's built-in voice.

Children

The lab is designed for children and does not ask for a name, email address, age or any other personal detail.

Hosting

Like any website, the company that hosts these pages can see basic technical request information, such as an IP address, in order to deliver them.

Changes

If optional grown-up accounts or other features are added later, this page will be updated before they launch.

Contact

Questions: reach You Noah Guy through younoahlab.com.
