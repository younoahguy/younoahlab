Privacy at You Noah Lab

Last updated November 25, 2026.

Who we are

You Noah Lab (younoahlab.com) is a science lab for children about 5 to 12 years old, run by You Noah Guy. You can reach us at younoahguy2026@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store or sell personal information about children or grown-ups.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no trackers, no analytics and no cookies, and the lab does not use your browser's storage.
  • Everything a child does in the lab stays in the browser tab and is gone when the tab is closed, unless a grown-up saves a file to their own device.
  • The few features that reach the internet are described below. The two that could send a child's own words (live AI answers and the microphone) stay off until a grown-up answers a grown-ups-only question.

What stays on your device

Experiments, badges, the Lab Journal, anything typed to NOAH, and the answers a grown-up gives in Make a learning plan (age, grade, learning needs and interests) are kept only in the open tab. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Closing the tab clears them. If you choose to save the journal or the learning plan, it is saved as a file on your own device, and only you have it.

Children under 13 (COPPA)

You Noah Lab is made for children. It does not ask a child for a name, email address, age, photo or any other personal detail, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The learning plan questions are for grown-ups, and their answers never leave the device. We do not show ads, sell data or let any third party track children on this site.

Please do not let children type names, addresses, school names or other personal details into NOAH, especially when live AI answers are on.

Your rights as a parent or guardian

Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws, you can ask what personal information about your child an operator holds, ask for it to be deleted, and refuse to allow further collection. Because we do not store anything about your child, we have nothing to show or delete. You can stop anything being sent at any time by turning sound off and leaving live answers and the microphone off. If you believe your child shared personal information with us, write to younoahguy2026@gmail.com and we will reply and help.

Schools and homeschool groups

The lab works without accounts, so a class can use it without sharing student information with us. Teachers can turn sound off and leave live answers and the microphone off, and then nothing is sent from the lab at all.

Changes

If we ever add something that collects information, such as optional grown-up accounts, we will update this page and ask for a parent's permission before it collects anything from a child.
